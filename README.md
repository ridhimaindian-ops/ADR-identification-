This session provides an overview of Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) and Pharmacovigilance, covering the definition and classification of ADRs, including pharmacological, pathological, genotoxic, allergic, idiosyncratic, pharmacogenetic, and teratogenic reactions. It also explains Type I–IV hypersensitivity reactions, teratogenic drug categories in pregnancy, and major causality assessment tools such as the Bradford Hill Criteria, WHO-UMC Scale, and Naranjo Algorithm. The session further introduces Pharmacovigilance and its role in patient safety and public health, along with practical guidance on ADR reporting through CDSCO forms and the ADR PvPI mobile app, and provides an overview of the Pharmacovigilance Programme of India (PvPI).
